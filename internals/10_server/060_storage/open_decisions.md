## Divergences

### #1

- decisions.md says: "`FreezeHandler` is a DECLARED exception: it uses `os`/`Path`/pickle directly instead of storage nodes."
- the implementation does what the decision states. `freeze_handler.py:66-71` imports `os`, `pickle`, `shutil` and `from pathlib import Path`; the semaphore is taken at `freeze_handler.py:148` with `fd = os.open(folder / LOCK_NAME, os.O_CREAT | os.O_EXCL | os.O_WRONLY, 0o600)`; the parcels are written at `freeze_handler.py:394` with `path.write_bytes(pickle.dumps({"header": header, "payload": payload}))` and read at `freeze_handler.py:398` with `return pickle.loads(path.read_bytes())`. The root is a plain path, never a storage node: `spa_commander.py:533` declares `frozen_users_path: str | Path,` and `spa_commander.py:550` does `self.freeze_handler = FreezeHandler(frozen_users_path)`; `worker_entry.py:110` does `self.frozen_users_path: str = self.config["frozen_users_path"]`. The word `Storage` in `freeze_handler.py` occurs only inside docstring prose (`:25`, `:112`, `:113`, `:117`): no import and no call.
- the test says: `tests/spa/orchestration/test_orchestration_freeze_handler.py::test_root_is_created_private` runs against a real `tmp_path` directory and asserts `os.stat(root).st_mode & 0o777 == 0o700`; the module docstring at `test_orchestration_freeze_handler.py:17-19` says "the deposit is the one place that talks to the filesystem directly, so a fake filesystem would assert nothing."
- the docstring says: `freeze_handler.py:24` "**The freezer only through this surface.** The house rule says the filesystem is reached through storage nodes; this class is the declared exception, and the exception is what buys it: the semaphore needs real exclusive creation (``O_CREAT|O_EXCL``), which no logical-volume surface offers."
- the handoff of 2026-08-19 says: "lo storage è sincrono via nodi, mai `await`; il freezer è l'eccezione dichiarata all'accesso diretto al filesystem."
- the design of 2026-08-16 says: "Il deposito sta dietro UNA classe (superficie stretta — la `FreezeHandler` di M1) … Il backend locale di QUESTA classe usa il filesystem DIRETTAMENTE — scritture DIRETTE sotto `.lock` … `os.open(O_CREAT|O_EXCL)` per il semaforo — in deroga dichiarata alla regola «solo storage node», riformulata: «il deposito solo via il nodo deposito»."
- the handoff of 2026-08-15 says the opposite: "`83ccb50` — il freezer parla storage node: `frozen_users_dir` è uno `StorageNode` (volumi logici, es. `GENROASGI:frozen_users`), parcel in CHIARO"; and in the section "Decisioni cementate in QUESTA sessione — NON riaprire": "**Filesystem SOLO via storage node, volumi logici** (es. `GENROASGI:`): «così se un domani si cambia idea va da un'altra parte»."
- the transcript `3e03d6d0-b821-4be1-9b0a-f6ad92b626f5.jsonl` of 2026-08-16 (user) says: "il deposito: l'unico punto in cui il progetto parla col filesystem con os.\* diretto (deroga F11 — «il deposito solo via il nodo deposito»)."
